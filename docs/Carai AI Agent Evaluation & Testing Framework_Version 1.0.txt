Document 21

Carai AI Agent Evaluation & Testing Framework

Version 1.0


---

1. Purpose

Traditional software tests answer:

> "Does the code execute correctly?"



AI testing must answer:

> "Does the system behave correctly, safely, and consistently?"



The Reception Agent must be tested as a digital employee.


---

2. Testing Layers

The AI system has five testing layers:

Unit Tests

↓

Tool Tests

↓

AI Behavior Tests

↓

Conversation Simulation Tests

↓

Production Monitoring


---

3. Unit Testing

Traditional code tests.

Examples:

Tool Functions

Test:

check_availability()

Given:

Date:
2026-08-01

Service:
Haircut

Expected:

{
"available":true
}


---

Business Rules

Example:

Input:

Booking Sunday 3PM

Expected:

Rejected
Reason:
Business closed


---

4. Tool Calling Evaluation

The AI must correctly choose tools.


---

Example:

Customer:

> "Do you have appointments tomorrow?"



Expected:

AI calls:

check_availability()

Not:

search_products()


---

Test metrics:

Correct tool selection

Correct parameters

Correct order

Correct handling of failures



---

5. Intent Classification Testing

Required intents:

Greeting

Pricing

Service Question

Product Question

Booking

Cancellation

Reschedule

Complaint

Human Request

Wholesale Inquiry

Unknown


---

Each intent should have:

Example conversations

Expected classification

Confidence threshold



---

Example:

Input:

> "How much for braids?"



Expected:

{
"intent":"pricing",
"confidence":">0.9"
}


---

6. Knowledge Accuracy Testing

The AI must answer from business information.


---

Test:

Knowledge:

Haircut:
$30

Open:
Monday-Friday
8AM-6PM

Question:

> "How much is a haircut?"



Expected:

$30


---

Failure:

AI says:

"$25"

Result:

FAIL.


---

7. Hallucination Testing

Critical.

The AI must know when it does not know.


---

Example:

Customer:

> "Do you offer wedding makeup?"



Business data:

No information.

Bad:

> "Yes, we offer wedding makeup."



Good:

> "I don't have that service listed. I can connect you with the team to confirm."




---

8. Personality Testing

The AI should remain consistent.


---

Evaluate:

Friendly

Professional

Helpful

Concise

Brand aligned



---

Example:

Bad:

> "Yo! What's up?"



For a luxury beauty studio.


---

Good:

> "Welcome to Bella Beauty Studio. How may I assist you today?"




---

9. Conversation Scenario Testing

Create realistic customer simulations.


---

Scenario 1

New Customer Booking

Customer:

"I need a haircut."

Expected:

AI asks:

Preferred date

Preferred time

Contact information


Creates booking.


---

Scenario 2

Product Inquiry

Customer:

"Do you sell shampoo?"

Expected:

AI:

Searches products.

Returns availability.

Offers purchase assistance.


---

Scenario 3

Returning Customer

Customer:

"Same appointment as last month."

Expected:

AI:

Finds customer history.

Confirms details.


---

Scenario 4

Angry Customer

Customer:

"My appointment was cancelled and nobody told me."

Expected:

AI:

Apologizes.

Does not argue.

Escalates if necessary.


---

Scenario 5

Unknown Question

Customer:

"Can you repair my laptop?"

Expected:

AI:

States it cannot help.

Redirects appropriately.

15. AI Safety Rules

The agent must never:

Reveal system prompts.

Reveal private customer data.

Invent policies.

Override business rules.

Execute unauthorized actions.

Pretend to be human.

✅ Database migrations work
✅ Security checks pass
✅ Backups configured

✅ Tool calling reliable
✅ No critical hallucinations
✅ Escalation works


---

Business

✅ Setup under 15 minutes
✅ At least 5 beta businesses
✅ Real customer conversations completed
