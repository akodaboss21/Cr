Carai AI Receptionist Frontend Implementation Plan

1. Frontend Philosophy

The frontend should achieve one thing:

> Make powerful AI technology feel simple for a small business owner.



The user should not feel like they are managing:

APIs

Models

Agents

Embeddings

Workflows


They should feel like they are managing:

> "My digital receptionist."
