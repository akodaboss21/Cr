PART 6 — Objection Handling


---

"AI won't understand my customers."

Response:

> That's exactly why Carai learns your business information, services, pricing, and policies. It is trained around your business, not a generic chatbot.




---

"I already answer messages myself."

Response:

> That's great, but the goal is not replacing you. It's helping you handle customers when you're busy, closed, or focused on clients.




---

"It's too expensive."

Response:

> One missed customer can already cost more than the monthly subscription. Carai is designed to recover those opportunities.




---

"Customers want humans."

Response:

> Customers still have the option for human help. Carai handles the simple questions and passes important situations to your team.
