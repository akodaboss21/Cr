Carai AI Receptionist UX/UI Specification

Version 1.0


---

UX Philosophy

Carai should feel like:

Professional business software

Simple enough for a salon owner

Powerful enough for enterprise teams


The user should never feel like they are "configuring AI."

They should feel like:

> "I hired a digital employee."

Simplicity

Visibility

Control



---

Speed
