Carai AI Receptionist V1 Go-To-Market & First 100 Customers Strategy

Version 1.0

Now we move from building → selling.

The objective:

> Get the first paying businesses, collect feedback, prove demand, and create the foundation for Admin ESA, DevOS, and the wider Carai ecosystem.



The mistake many AI startups make is trying to sell "AI".

Businesses do not buy AI.

They buy:

More customers

Faster responses

Less missed revenue

Less repetitive work

More organization



---

1. Initial Market Position

Product Positioning

Do not position as:

❌ "An AI agent platform"

Too technical.


---

Position as:

✅ "Your 24/7 AI receptionist for your business."


---

Core promise:

> Carai answers your customers, captures leads, and helps you book more appointments while you focus on your business.




---

2. Initial Target Market Priority

Your chosen markets:

1. Hair salons


2. Barbershops


3. Beauty studios


4. Nail salons


5. Wholesale/Retail stores



Good choices because they have:

Repetitive questions

Appointment needs

Customer messaging

High missed-message costs

Owner-operated businesses

7. Demo Strategy

Never demo features.

Demo a transformation.


---

Bad demo:

"Here is our AI model."


---

Good demo:

Before:

Customer:

"Hi, how much is braiding?"

Owner:

Busy. No reply.

Customer leaves.


---

After:

Customer:

"Hi, how much is braiding?"

Carai:

"Welcome! Our braiding starts at $80. Would you like to see available appointments?"


---

Business owner understands instantly.


---

Visit:
