Carai AI Receptionist

Product Specification v1.0

Product Mission

> Carai AI Receptionist is a digital receptionist that works 24/7 to answer customers, capture leads, schedule appointments, and help businesses never miss an opportunity.



It is the first product in the Carai ecosystem and the first business module that will eventually connect to Admin ESA.


---

Product Goals

The AI Receptionist should help businesses:

Never miss a customer.

Answer repetitive questions instantly.

Reduce phone calls.

Increase bookings.

Capture every lead.

Save owners time.

Improve customer satisfaction.

Guiding Principles

Every design and engineering decision should be measured against these principles:

1. Solve a real business problem first.


2. Be usable by non-technical business owners.


3. Remain modular so it can become part of Admin ESA later.


4. Expose APIs and extension points for DevOS and future products.


5. Scale from a single business to thousands of organizations without architectural changes.
