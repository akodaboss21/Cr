3. Tool Framework

This is probably the biggest missing piece.

We already know the Reception Agent uses tools.

Now define every tool.

Example

book_appointment()

cancel_appointment()

search_products()

search_services()

search_customer()

search_inventory()

create_lead()

update_customer()

notify_staff()

Every tool should define:

Description

Inputs

Outputs

Permissions

Validation

Audit events

Error responses

Retry behavior

Idempotency


Think of this as your internal "tool SDK."

5. Business Rule Engine

One thing I think will make Carai stand out is separating AI from business rules.

Example:

Business closes at 6 PM.

↓

Booking after 6 PM?

↓

Rule Engine

↓

Reject booking

↓

AI explains why.

Never let the AI decide business rules.
